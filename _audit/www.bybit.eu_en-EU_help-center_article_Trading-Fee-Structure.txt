Bybit EU Trading Fee Structure
Last updated on 2026-08-04 13:33:53
Share
Bybit EU trading fees are different for Non-VIP and VIP users. Users can enjoy higher trading fee discounts according to different VIP levels.
The VIP level will be determined based on your asset balance or last 30-day trading volume. Traders are only required to meet one of the criteria to unlock the fee discount of the respective VIP Level. For example, Trader A has a 250,000 EUR asset balance (VIP 2 criteria), and his last 30-day Spot trading volume has reached 10,000,000 EUR (VIP 3 criteria). Trader A will be promoted to VIP Level 3 and will be entitled to all discounted fee rates stated under VIP 3.
The VIP level will be up dated daily at 7AM UTC and the Subaccounts will have the fee rate of the Main Account. Please note that the trading fee rates applied on your orders will be the ones applicable on your account at the time of order placement.
