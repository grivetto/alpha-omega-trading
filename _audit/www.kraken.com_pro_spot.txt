How to trade spot on Kraken Pro
Fund your account - Deposit local currency or crypto to your Kraken account.
Choose a pair - Select the market you want to trade, then review price, order-book depth, and recent activity.
Set your order - Pick an order type, then enter size and review the fee before confirming.
Trade and settle - Your order executes at the market price and the asset settles to your balance, owned outright and ready to hold, withdraw, or trade again.
