Binance: Restricts MiCA Services Only in Six EEA States
Binance limits MiCA restrictions to Italy, Spain, France, Poland, Belgium and Sweden while EEA users elsewhere continue sign-ups and trading.
Source
Analysis
Binance has restricted new sign-ups, deposits and Earn products solely in Italy, Spain, France, Poland, Belgium and Sweden after MiCA took full effect, leaving the remainder of the EEA unaffected and open for trading. The selective rollout contradicts ESMA guidance and shows the exchange prioritising revenue over uniform MiCA regulation impact across borders. Users in non-listed EEA jurisdictions retain full access, exposing uneven Binance EU compliance and raising enforcement questions for cross-border crypto rules.
