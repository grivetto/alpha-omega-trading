# Denaro Strategies Knowledge Base

## Gaussian Channel
- Detects breakout/mean-reversion using a Gaussian Channel (mean ± std).
- Generates bullish/bearish signals based on price crossing upper/lower bands.
- Used for grid trading entries.

## AI Signal Generator
- Consumes market data (order book, price series) and applies statistical or ML models.
- Produces a probability score and trade direction.
- Designed for integration with grid or spot bots.

## Arbitrage MEV Scanner
- Monitors price differences across multiple exchanges for the same asset.
- Executes atomic swaps or flash loans to capture spread.
- Focus on low-latency and high-frequency execution.

## AI Portfolio Optimizer
- Allocates capital across assets using Kelly criterion or risk‑adjusted metrics.
- Periodically rebalances based on performance and volatility.
- Outputs target allocation percentages.

## Self‑Improvement Loop
- Daily review of video content (YouTube, streams) and extraction of new ideas.
- Back‑testing of newly discovered strategies on historical data.
- Promotion of validated strategies to live trading after risk checks.
